ADELAIDE'S ACCOUNT

Edwin's physician. Among other things.

Dr. Adelaide Vale admits that her relationship with Edwin had become more than professional. She also confirms something few people at the party knew: Edwin was dying.

Years of drinking had destroyed his liver. According to Adelaide, his condition had advanced considerably and she believed he had only weeks left to live.

She describes Edwin as increasingly sick and in considerable pain, but says he continued drinking despite her warnings.

Adelaide says she saw Edwin leave the Dining Room after his argument with Poe, but denies following him. She says she eventually made her way to the Drawing Room, where she sat at the piano in an attempt to escape the party for a while.

That is where Elizabeth found her.

“You play the piano about as poorly as you've hid your secrets, Dr. Vale. I'm not so upset about what that pig does to you as I am that it was happening in my own home. You have no decency, you harlot!”

Adelaide doesn't deny the confrontation. She insists Elizabeth had known about the affair and had every reason to despise both of them.

When asked about the pale powder along the hem of her dress, Adelaide brushes at it and says she must have picked up some fireplace ash earlier in the evening.

Adelaide also points to the peculiar circumstances of Edwin's death. After publicly accusing Poe of committing the acts described in his own stories, Edwin was found hidden behind a wall in a manner remarkably similar to The Black Cat.

She says that if investigators want to know what Poe was doing after the argument, Evelyn Whitmore seemed unusually interested in exactly that.

INVESTIGATION NOTES

Adelaide states:

Edwin was suffering from advanced liver disease and likely had only weeks to live.

She and Edwin were having an affair.

She saw Edwin leave the Dining Room after his argument with Poe.

She later went to the Drawing Room, where Elizabeth confronted her.

The pale powder on her dress is fireplace ash.

She denies following Edwin after he left the Dining Room.

FIND EVELYN WHITMORE Adelaide says Evelyn was particularly interested in Poe's movements after the argument.
