ADELAIDE'S ACCOUNT

Edwin's physician. Among other things.

Dr. Adelaide Vale sighs and admits that her relationship with Edwin had become more than doctor/patient. She also confirms something few people at the party knew: Edwin wasn't just sick, he was dying.

Years of drinking had destroyed his liver. According to Adelaide, his condition had advanced considerably and she believed he had only weeks left to live.

Regarding the happenings that night, Adelaide says that after the argument, she followed Edwin into the Study because he looked particularly unwell. She found him lying on the fainting couch, coughing up blood.

She says she checked his temperature and remained with him briefly until his coughing subsided. Adelaide says she left him alone in the Study to rest and eventually made her way to the Drawing Room.

That is where Elizabeth found her and made cruel, and heated remarks about their affair, which Adelaide had no idea she was aware of.

Adelaide doesn't deny the confrontation and says Elizabeth was furious about the affair and had every reason to despise both of them.

Adelaide states that she thinks Elizabeth, who never showed love to Edwin, was, in her professional opinion, angry about the affair and killed him.

You notice a pale powder along the hem of her dress, Adelaide brushes at it and says it's ash from the fireplace.

Adelaide also points to the peculiar circumstances of Edwin's death. After publicly accusing Poe of committing the acts described in his own stories, Edwin was found hidden behind a wall in a manner remarkably similar to The Black Cat.

She says that if investigators want to know what Poe was doing after the argument, Evelyn Whitmore seemed unusually interested in exactly that.

INVESTIGATION NOTES

Adelaide states:

Edwin was suffering from advanced liver disease and likely had only weeks to live.

She and Edwin were having an affair.

She saw Edwin leave the Dining Room after his argument with Poe.

She later went to the Drawing Room, where Elizabeth confronted her.

The pale powder on her dress is fireplace ash.

She denies following Edwin after he left the Dining Room.

FIND EVELYN WHITMORE Adelaide says Evelyn was particularly interested in Poe's movements after the argument.
