SAMUEL'S ACCOUNT

He seems considerably less concerned about this than you are.

Samuel seems delighted to learn that his fingerprints were found on the candlestick. In fact, he admits almost immediately that he handled it.

Samuel says he was in the Dining Room when he noticed Evelyn Whitmore slip away into the Service Passage. Curious about where she was going, he followed her shortly afterward.

By the time Samuel entered the passage, Evelyn was nowhere to be seen. Instead, he encountered Elizabeth coming from the Kitchen.

Samuel says he offered Elizabeth a polite bow. She responded with something he describes simply as a “hmph” before continuing into the Dining Room.

With Evelyn gone and apparently nothing more interesting happening in the passage, Samuel noticed the Boiler Room and decided to look inside.

That is where he found the brass candlestick lying on the floor.

Samuel says it immediately caught his attention because the candlestick was unusually clean compared with the dusty room around it. He picked it up, examined it, and then placed it back where he found it.

“So those are my fingerprints? How wonderfully inconvenient.”

Samuel insists the candlestick was already inside the Boiler Room when he arrived. He says he never entered Edwin's Study and has no idea how the candlestick got there.

INVESTIGATION NOTES

Samuel states:

He saw Evelyn leave the Dining Room and enter the Service Passage.

He followed her shortly afterward, but she was already gone.

He encountered Elizabeth coming from the Kitchen before entering the Boiler Room.

The brass candlestick was already lying on the Boiler Room floor when he arrived.

He picked up and examined the candlestick, explaining his fingerprints.

He says he never entered Edwin's Study.

FIND PLUTO Someone else was moving through the house that evening with blood on their hands.
