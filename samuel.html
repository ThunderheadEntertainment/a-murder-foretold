SAMUEL'S ACCOUNT

Samuel seems considerably less concerned about this than you are.

Samuel seems delighted to learn that their fingerprints were found on the candlestick and smiles warmly at you while you ask about it.

Samuel says they were in the Dining Room when they noticed Evelyn Whitmore slip away into the Service Passage. Curious about how Evelyn snuck out and where she was going, Samuel followed shortly afterward.

By the time Samuel entered the service passage, Evelyn was nowhere to be seen. Instead, they encountered Elizabeth coming from the Kitchen.

Samuel says they offered Elizabeth a polite bow. She responded with something Samuel describes simply as a hostile adjusting of her dress, a “hmph”, and a nose in the air before continuing into the Dining Room.

After Elizabeth left, Samuel was still curious as to where Evelyn went, so they turned to their left and opened the door to the Boiler Room.

That is where they found the brass candlestick lying on the floor.

Samuel says it immediately caught their attention because the candlestick was unusually clean compared with the dusty room around it. They picked it up, examined it, and then placed it back where they found it, smiling to themselves as it was only a matter of time before humans found themselves immersed in intrigue and drama.

Samuel states they then went back into the dining room, awaiting the inevitable theatrics that would follow.
“So those are my fingerprints. How wonderfully inconvenient for me, no?”

Samuel states the candlestick was already inside the Boiler Room when they arrived. They say they never entered Edwin's Study and have no idea how the candlestick got there.

INVESTIGATION NOTES

Samuel states:

They saw Evelyn leave the Dining Room and enter the Service Passage.

They followed shortly afterward, but Evelyn was already gone.

They encountered Elizabeth coming from the Kitchen and leave to the dining room.

Upon entering the Boiler Room, the brass candlestick was already lying on the floor.

Samuel picked up and examined the candlestick.

They say they never entered Edwin's Study.

FIND PLUTO Someone else was moving through the house that evening with blood on their hands.
