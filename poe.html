POE'S ACCOUNT

Well, he certainly has a temper.

You question Poe about what happened after his very public argument with Edwin.

Poe makes no attempt to hide his disdain for the man, even as he lays dead only a few doors away. Poe denies killing Edwin and tells you that after leaving the Dining Room, he went directly to the Library to be alone, but after a few seconds, Pluto entered and inquired as to his wellbeing. Poe states that he remembered telling Pluto "yes, thank you old friend. Though you are a spirit and your presence haunts my abashed heart with regret and melancholy, I am buffeted by your forgiveness and concern. I would ask you for one more favor, friend, and that is for a small bit of solitude at the moment." Pluto obliged and exited the room towards the outdoor garden.

Alone in the Library and still stewing over the argument, Poe admits that he continued arguing with Edwin aloud, but in the empty room. Poe recounts the words he angrily hissed at the imaginary Edwin:

“Perhaps I should kill you tonight and write the story in the morning. Would that satisfy you, Edwin?! How about THAT!”

Poe insists Edwin wasn't actually there. He says he was alone, taking his anger out on the fireplace logs with an iron poker, thumping and stabbing the logs over and over, then angrily throwing the poker to the floor.

Eventually Poe cooled off, straightened himself up, and headed back toward the party.

While crossing the Great Hall, however, he overheard an argument coming from the Drawing Room. Elizabeth Graves was giving Dr. Adelaide Vale an earful. Poe couldn't make out every word, but he heard enough to understand the subject of their argument.

A JUICY LITTLE DETAIL

Apparently Dr. Adelaide Vale was having an affair with Edwin Graves. Poe overheard Elizabeth say something to the effect of "don't think I don't know what you and Edwin have been doing in my own home!"

Poe shrugged and says "Edwin was an unwell, and apparently adulterous, man who met a nasty fate. He is the reaper of what he had himself sown."

It seems like if you can speak to Elizabeth, perhaps you can start to verify parts of Poe's alibi. FIND Elizabeth
