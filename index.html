A MURDER FORETOLD

A Raven's Ball Murder Mystery

The Raven's Ball, the annual celebration of Edgar Allan Poe was already off to a contentious start.

Edwin Graves, who already had a tense relationship with Poe, was selected by shared social circles to host the event. Graves, drunk and belligerent, had spent much of the evening coughing and not-so-subtly implying that Edgar Allan Poe was not a fiction writer at all, but rather an author specializing in autobiographical events. After enough brandy had loosened his tongue, Edwin loudly confronted Poe in the dining room, yelling that Poe's stories weren't fiction at all, but rather confessions of crimes he had truly, and cruelly, committed. Graves even went so far as to point out that Poe had killed his black cat Pluto, just like in his fictional story, 'The Black Cat'.

Embarrassed, Poe lost his temper and snapped back.

“Maybe I should do as you say and kill you tonight, then write about it in the morning, Mr. Graves! I even have a title for it! 'A Bloody Drunken Idiot'!”

The two men stormed off in opposite directions. Edwin to his Study while Poe disappeared into the Library.

The party returned to its usual drinking, dancing and gossipping about the scandal and drama unfolding before them.

A scream cut through the din of the ball.

A kitchen server stood in terror as part of an unfinished masonry wall was collapsed and strewn across the floor.

Among the broken stone and wet mortar was the body of Edwin Graves, his skull crushed, malformed, and bloody.

Standing over him was Pluto, Poe's infamous black cat, smiling, with both hands soaked in fresh blood.
