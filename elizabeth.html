ELIZABETH'S ACCOUNT

The grieving widow. More or less.

Elizabeth appears angry. Combative. She confirms that she knew about Edwin's affair with Dr. Adelaide Vale with a smirk. She makes no attempt to hide her contempt for either of them. "They think me a fool, but she took that rancid pig from my bed, so I have only benefitted from all of this."

Elizabeth states that after Edwin's outburst, she thought the room needed less sobriety and went to the kitchen, which was empty when she arrived. A server entered immediately behind her.

Elizabeth snapped at the server to get more champagne for the guests and when the server left, she poured herself a glass in silence. She says spilled her first glass, cursed aloud, then poured another. She then removed a large chef's knife from the block to slice a strawberry for her drink. She left half the strawberry on the counter and left the kitchen back toward the Service Passage.

From the passage, Elizabeth looked left and saw Evelyn Whitmore disappearing into Edwin's Study.

A second later, Samuel, the strange being who appeared at the party without an invite and seemed unbothered by it, entered the passage from the Dining Room. Samuel bowed. Elizabeth did not return the courtesy and went back into the Dining Room, where she was greeted by the sounds of someone loudly murdering the piano in the drawing room.

She stormed into the drawing room and found Dr. Adelaide Vale at the piano.

“You play the piano about as poorly as you've hid your secrets, Dr. Vale. I'm not so upset about what that pig does to you as I am that it was happening in my own home. You have no decency and quite honestly I question your taste in men!”

Elizabeth admits that Edwin's death leaves her a wealthy widow. She denies having anything to do with his murder.

INVESTIGATION NOTES

Elizabeth saw Evelyn enter the Study, Samuel in the Service Passage, and Adelaide in the Drawing Room.

Elizabeth admits handling a large chef's knife shortly before Edwin was found.
