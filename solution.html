YOU SOLVED IT

The truth behind A Murder Foretold

If you're reading this, you opened the safe.

Which means you correctly identified Dr. Adelaide Vale as Edwin Graves' killer, the brass candlestick as the murder weapon, and mercy as the motive.

WHAT REALLY HAPPENED

After Edwin's confrontation with Poe, he retreated to the Study. Adelaide followed. Edwin was gravely ill, drunk, coughing blood, and suffering from the final stages of liver disease.

Adelaide loved him. She also knew that he had very little time left.

She found Edwin lying on the fainting couch and gave him a powerful injection, followed by another, intending to quietly end his suffering.

Edwin became unresponsive.

Adelaide believed she had killed him.

Then she panicked.

A newspaper containing Poe's The Black Cat gave her an idea. Edwin had spent the evening publicly accusing Poe of committing the horrors described in his stories. If Edwin were found murdered in exactly that fashion, suspicion would fall naturally on Poe.

Adelaide took one of the matching brass candlesticks from the Study and dragged Edwin into the adjoining Scullery, leaving the smear of brandy across the Study floor.

She struck Edwin repeatedly in the back of the head with the candlestick and concealed his body behind the unfinished masonry wall.

There was only one problem with Adelaide's plan: Edwin wasn't dead yet.

The injections had rendered Edwin unconscious, but they had not killed him. The blows Adelaide intended only to disguise his death were what actually ended his life.

Adelaide escaped through the Kitchen and into the Boiler Room, where she wiped the candlestick clean and abandoned it. Evelyn nearly caught her leaving.

Samuel discovered the candlestick later, picked it up and examined it, unknowingly leaving the fingerprints that would make Samuel appear guilty.

The pale powder on Adelaide's dress wasn't fireplace ash at all. It was mortar from the wall she had used to conceal Edwin's body.

Meanwhile, Pluto's bloody hands came from an unfortunate raven, Elizabeth's knife had sliced nothing more sinister than a strawberry, and Poe's murderous threats and violent thumping in the Library were directed entirely at a pile of fireplace logs.
