THE KITCHEN

Examination of the scene

You examine the Kitchen and find several items that seem to be consistent with Elizabeth's account.

OBSERVATIONS

A large chef's knife sits on the counter, removed from the knife block.

The blade is wet with strawberry juice. No blood is present.

Half of a freshly sliced strawberry remains on the counter.

A small champagne spill remains on the Kitchen floor.

On the inside of the door to the Garden is a bloody smear. There is no blood on the interior door handle, but there is blood on the exterior door handle.

Kitchen staff confirm that the room was empty when Elizabeth entered. A server followed her inside moments later to retrieve more champagne.
