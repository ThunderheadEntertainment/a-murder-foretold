PLUTO'S ACCOUNT

Yes, that's blood. No, it's not his.

Pluto makes no attempt to deny having threatened Edwin earlier in the evening. Edwin had attacked Poe, and Pluto admits that they wanted him punished for it.

After Poe left the Dining Room, Pluto followed him into the Library. Poe was furious, but eventually asked Pluto to leave him alone. Pluto obliged and exited the house into the Garden.

While wandering outside, Pluto passed the window to Edwin's Study and looked inside.

Edwin was lying on the fainting couch. Dr. Adelaide Vale was standing over him.

Pluto says Adelaide produced a syringe and gave Edwin an injection. Pluto thought little of it. Adelaide was Edwin's physician, and Edwin was obviously ill.

Pluto continued through the Garden, where something considerably more interesting caught their attention: a raven.

“It was a bird. I am a cat. Surely I don't have to explain this part.”

Pluto chased, killed and ate the raven. The blood covering Pluto's hands is raven blood.

Pluto eventually returned to the house through the Garden door into the Kitchen. With bloody hands, they pushed the door closed behind them and continued toward the Scullery.

As Pluto approached, there was a sudden crash from inside the Scullery. Part of the unfinished masonry wall had collapsed.

Behind it was Edwin Graves.

Pluto approached the body just before the others arrived and discovered them standing over Edwin with fresh blood still covering their hands.

INVESTIGATION NOTES

Pluto states:

Poe was alone in the Library when Pluto left through the Garden.

Through the Study window, Pluto saw Adelaide standing over Edwin.

Pluto saw Adelaide give Edwin an injection.

The blood on Pluto's hands came from a raven killed in the Garden.

Pluto later reentered the house through the Kitchen's Garden door.

The Scullery wall collapsed as Pluto approached, revealing Edwin's body.
