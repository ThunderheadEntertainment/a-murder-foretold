THE BOILER ROOM

Examination of the scene

You examine the Boiler Room after Evelyn reports seeing its door close from the Service Passage.

OBSERVATIONS

A brass candlestick lies on the floor near the doorway.

It appears to match the candlestick remaining in Edwin's Study.

Unlike nearly everything else in the Boiler Room, the candlestick is remarkably clean.

A closer examination reveals traces of blood inside a seam near its base.

Fingerprints recovered from the candlestick belong to Samuel.

Samuel's fingerprints are on the candlestick. FIND SAMUEL
