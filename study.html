THE STUDY

Examination of the scene

Edwin was last seen entering his Study after his argument with Poe.

OBSERVATIONS

No blood or signs of a violent struggle.

A broad smear of liquid that reeks of brandy runs across the floor toward the Scullery.

A shoe print crosses through the brandy smear in the opposite direction, entering the Study. A bloody handkerchief lays near a fainting couch. A single candelabra sits off-centered on a table, as if it's missing its twin.

This is all you find in the room. Perhaps it's time to question Elizabeth after what Poe said about Edwin's affair.
