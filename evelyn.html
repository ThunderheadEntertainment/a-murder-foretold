EVELYN'S ACCOUNT

Poe's ever-watchful publicist.

Evelyn confirms that she entered Edwin's Study. After Poe and Edwin's argument, she wanted to know what Poe was doing before his temper created an even larger problem for her to manage.

Rather than follow Poe through the Great Hall, Evelyn slipped into the Service Passage. As she passed the Kitchen, she heard a commotion and cracked open the door.

Inside, she saw Elizabeth Graves pull a large knife from the block.

A creak behind her caught her attention. Evelyn turned and saw the Boiler Room door closing. She did not see who was behind it.

Evelyn continued to Edwin's Study. Upon entering, she slipped in a wet smear on the floor, catching herself before she fell.

THE FOOTPRINT

Evelyn confirms that the shoe print through the brandy smear in the Study is hers.

From the Study, Evelyn pressed herself against the wall adjoining the Library and listened.

She heard Poe speaking angrily:

“Perhaps I should kill you tonight and write the story in the morning. Would that satisfy you, Edwin?! How about THAT!”

Immediately afterward, Evelyn heard several heavy thuds followed by a metallic crash. She never saw what was happening inside the Library.

Evelyn insists she never encountered Edwin in the Study.

INVESTIGATION NOTES

Evelyn states:

She saw Elizabeth handling a large knife in the Kitchen.

She saw the Boiler Room door close, but did not see who was inside.

The shoe print crossing the brandy smear in the Study belongs to her.

She heard Poe threatening Edwin and several heavy impacts from inside the Library.

She never saw Edwin in the Study.

EXAMINE THE BOILER ROOM Evelyn saw someone close the Boiler Room door.
